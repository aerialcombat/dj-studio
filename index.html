DJ Studio

DJ Studio is a private publishing tool. It uploads short videos made by its owner's studio to YouTube channels that the owner runs, and schedules them using YouTube's own scheduling.

It is used only by its owner, for the owner's own channels. It is not offered to the public, and it does not access anyone else's account.

What it does with YouTube

  • Uploads videos, with their title, description, tags and publish time, to a channel the owner has signed in.
  • Reads the signed-in channel's name and ID, to confirm the upload goes to the right channel.
